Choosing an online Quran teacher is one of the most important decisions you will make for your own learning — or for your child’s. The right tutor does far more than read along: they listen to every letter, correct gently, and keep you moving forward week after week. And for many sisters, the right teacher is, quite simply, a woman — which is why a female Quran teacher online can make all the difference.

This guide explains what to look for, which questions to ask in a demo class, and how we match students with teachers at Online Taleem ul Quran.

## Why the teacher matters more than the platform

The Quran has always been taught from person to person: the teacher recites, the student repeats, and the teacher corrects. Apps and videos can help you practise, but they cannot hear your mistakes. A live teacher can — and the small corrections they make in every class are what turn hesitant reading into confident recitation.

That is why, when you compare online Quran classes, it is worth looking past the website and asking a simpler question: *who will actually be listening to me recite?*

## Seven things to look for in an online Quran teacher

### 1. Strong Tajweed — and the habit of correcting it

A good teacher recites correctly and, just as importantly, notices when *you* don’t. In every class you should be reciting more than your teacher, and hearing clear, specific corrections: which letter, what went wrong, how to fix it. Our [Simplified Tajweed course](/course-details/simplified-tajweed) is taught by male and female teachers — including Huffaz, Mujawwids and Qaris — with wide experience of teaching online.

### 2. Patience with beginners

Many students arrive a little nervous, especially adults who feel they “should already know this”. A good teacher is calm and encouraging, never embarrassed by the basics, and happy to repeat a letter as many times as it takes.

### 3. A clear path from Qaida to Quran

Ask how you will progress. At our academy, students complete the Mastery Phase of the Al-Burhan Qaida before moving on to Tajweed (taught in three levels), fluent [Quran recitation](/course-details/quran-recitation) and, if they wish, [Quran memorization](/course-details/quran-memorization). A teacher who can explain the path is a teacher with a plan.

### 4. Attention that suits you

One-to-one classes give you the teacher’s full attention and are ideal for Tajweed and Hifz. Batch classes suit students who enjoy learning alongside others. We offer both, so you can choose what fits.

### 5. Regular progress checks

Progress should be visible. Our monthly exams give students, parents and teachers a clear picture of what has been mastered and what to work on next.

### 6. Timings that fit your life

The best teacher is one you can actually attend. We offer flexible timings on weekdays and weekends, and missed classes can be rescheduled based on availability.

### 7. A free demo before you commit

You should never have to guess. A free demo class lets you meet the teacher, have your reading assessed and see how the lessons work before you decide.

## Questions to ask in your free demo class

Take a few minutes during the demo to ask:

- How will you correct my pronunciation, and how often?
- Where do you think I should start — Qaida, Tajweed or recitation?
- How will I know I am making progress?
- What should I practise between classes, and for how long?
- Can classes move if my schedule changes?

A good online Quran tutor will be glad you asked.

## Warning signs to watch for

Be cautious if a teacher does most of the reciting while you listen, rarely corrects mistakes, rushes through pages to show “progress”, or cannot explain what you will learn next. Be wary, too, of being asked for a long commitment before you have tried a single class. With us, you pay for what you learn.
